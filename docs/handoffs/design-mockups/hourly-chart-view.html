Hourly: a chart view beside the list

The list stays the default. A switch beside the chips turns the same day into the charts Hourly had before the rows (#667): temperature and humidity, sun, rain, wind. The chips filter the charts the way they filter the list's columns. Drag across any chart to read an hour.

Where the chart lives

  • A, a switch (my recommendation). Two icons in one pill at the end of the chip row: list or chart. The chips stay the same five, and keep their meaning in both.
  • All stacks the four charts as they were, one drag reading every chart at once, each with its own figures for that hour above it.
  • Temp, Rain, Wind or Sun shows that chart alone, taller, with the read hour's figures in tiles under it, the same figures an opened row gives.
  • The switch and the chip are remembered on the device, as the view is now.
  • B, no switch. The chosen chip's chart sits short above the rows, and the rows keep scrolling under it. All shows the temperature chart.
  • Dragging the chart opens that hour's row, so the chart is a way into the list rather than a second screen.
  • The cost: about 150 px of every Hourly visit goes to a chart, and the list starts lower. No view of all four charts at once.

What each chart keeps from before

  • Temperature and humidity: the air line coloured by each hour's score, the likely range shaded behind it, dew point dashed, humidity in orchid, the good hours shaded, sky every three hours. One scale, at the left (owner, 2026-10-27).
  • Sun: minutes of sun per hour on 0 to 60, cloud cover as the grey behind, night named once.
  • Rain: drawn as C, your 2026-11-24 pick: a row of chance cells on top, darker the likelier, figures from 30% up; amount bars per hour on a printed inch scale below. Per hour, not threes, as the rows are (owner, 2026-11-24). An unanswered chance is a dashed cell, never a pale one. Thu has the shower; Sun is dry all day.
  • Wind: sustained as the line, gusts dashed, the likely range shaded.
  • Today draws a dashed “now” line. A missing figure is a break in the line or a dash in the chip (Fri 3 AM wind).

Decisions this needs from you

  • A or B.
  • The slim header follows the drag again in chart mode, as it did before #667; in list mode it follows the opened row. One read hour is shared, so switching modes keeps your place.
  • Rain range left out. Your Range pick shades driest to wettest model, and per-model hourly rain is not on /hourly yet. This draws the median only until that field exists.
  • Card order. The old charts could be reordered (CardStack). I left that out: All draws them in one fixed order. Say if you want the reorder back.
