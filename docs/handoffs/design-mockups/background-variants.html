A · Current

  • Near black, #07090c, under a teal glow at the top that fades out by mid-screen. Most of the screen is the near-black.
  • Highest contrast of the five, and the reason it reads as very dark: the ground reflects about 0.3% of white.
  • On OLED phones a near-black ground can smear when scrolling (pixels switching off and on), and bright text on it can glow at the edges (halation). Material's dark theme uses #121212 rather than black for both reasons.

B · Charcoal

  • Neutral dark grey, #15181c, close to Material's #121212 recommendation. About three times the light of A.
  • Plain and calm. It drops the blue-black tint, so the teal brand and the lime scores carry all the colour.
  • The glow at the top is nearly gone; the ground is close to flat.

C · Slate

  • Blue-grey, #161d26. Keeps today's cool tint, lifted to about four times A's light.
  • Reads as night sky rather than black. The rain blue sits closer to the ground, so rain figures stand out a little less than on B or D.

D · Deep teal

  • Dark teal, #0f1d21, with the top glow kept. About four times A's light.
  • The ground takes the brand hue, so the app has one identity instead of black plus an accent. The lime scores and amber and red still separate clearly from it.
  • My pick: it answers "too dark" without going grey, and it keeps the one thing in the current look that was chosen for this app (the teal glow).

E · Dim

  • Lifted slate, #1d242d, about six times A's light. The softest of the five; closest to a "dimmed" theme.
  • Hairlines and tiles need bigger steps to show, and the red score on a tile drops to 4.9:1, the nearest to the floor of any option.
  • In direct sun on the crag, a lighter dark ground loses some punch. Size and contrast carry sunlight readability, not darkness.

What every option changes: the ground, the glow, the surface, tile and hairline steps, and the inactive tab grey (lifted in B–E so it stays over 4.5:1 on a tile; today's measures 4.2:1 there). The phone's status bar colour follows the ground. Scores, text and the brand teal are untouched, and all of them stay well over the 4.5:1 floor in every option.

Not drawn: a light theme that follows the phone's setting. That is a bigger change (every chart and colour needs a light version, and lime on white fails contrast), so it would be its own decision.

Cost of B–E: about seven token values in packages/design, then a check:ui pass to look at every screen. No layout or data change.
