Sandstone, early November: about 7 hours a day with the sun above 10°. "Sun hours" is the day's forecast sunshine divided by a cloudless day's, times those 7 hours. It's the scoring model's (GFS) number. Later days add the spread across the other models. No watts and no percentages; the hourly band shows the same share without a number.
