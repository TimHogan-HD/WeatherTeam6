Yellow: minutes of the hour the sun is out bright enough to cast a shadow. Grey behind: how much of the sky is cloud. Strength is the sunlight reaching flat ground: strong is 700 W/m² and up (a clear summer midday), weak is under 300. Terrain shade and a wall’s angle are not in it, and past day 3 an hour’s sun is a rough guess.

What it follows

  • Sun minutes are Open-Meteo's sunshine_duration (WMO: direct sun ≥ 120 W/m²) from the scoring model, GFS. A bar owns the hour before its stamp, on a fixed 0–60 scale.
  • A gap is never "no sun": an unanswered daylight hour is a dashed mark, an unanswered day is Sun — and has no icon. A measured sunless hour is a faint stub.
  • Windows are runs of hours with at least 30 min of sun. Up to two are listed; more read "on and off".
  • "Forecasts differ" when GFS, ECMWF and ICON's day totals spread 3 h or more (GEM never: #155). compare:sky set the 3 h.
  • Strength is a word with its W/m² in the readout only. An hour with light but no bright sun reads "through cloud".
  • The cloud bars are faded on purpose (1.47:1, 2.28:1 at the read hour); the readout prints the exact %.
  • UV is left out: it tracks heating poorly at low sun.
