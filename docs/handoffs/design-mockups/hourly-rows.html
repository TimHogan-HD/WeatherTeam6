    Hourly as rows, one reading at a time

    The Hourly tab today stacks four charts: temperature and humidity, sun, rain, wind. This replaces them with one list of hours and a picker for which reading the rows show. Tap an hour to see every figure for it.

    Picker

    • A, the dropdown you described. A native select, so the phone opens its own wheel. Two taps per change, and the other views are hidden until you open it.
    • B, chips (my recommendation). The same five views, all visible, one tap each. They fit in one row at 390 px. With five options a dropdown hides choices that fit on screen anyway; it earns its place past about seven.
    • Either way the view is remembered on the device, like the card order is now.

    What each row keeps

    • Hour, sky and score never move. Changing the view swaps only the right-hand columns, so your eye stays on the same hour.
    • All is the default: temperature, chance of rain, wind. That answers most looks without touching the picker.
    • Each single view adds a bar on a scale printed in the column head (the day's low to high, 0 to 0.10 in, 0 to 40 mph, 0 to 60 min of sun), so a row still reads as a chart when you scan down it.
    • Good hours carry a faint green band, named once above the list. Hours already gone today fold behind “Show earlier hours”.
    • A missing figure prints a dash (Fri 3 AM wind, Sat 11 PM score).

    Decisions this needs from you

    • Rain per hour, not per three hours. Your 2026-10-30 call put Hourly's rain in three-hour blocks because a hidden scale made a trace look like a downpour. Here every row prints its inches and the bar's scale is in the column head, so I think per-hour is safe. Say if you want threes kept: the Rain view would group rows by three with one figure each.
    • The scrub goes. The slim header follows the hour you opened instead of the hour under your finger.
    • Daily stays as it is. A Daily row still opens that day here.
