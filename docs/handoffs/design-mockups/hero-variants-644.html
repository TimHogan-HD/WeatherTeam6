1 · As picked

  • The baseline: variant C's hero, a number and three short lines.
  • It answers "is it good now", but the line is still three dot-separated strings, which #644 asks to turn into label and value pairs.
  • Nothing in it is specific to this crag except the rock type.

2 · Figure on a rule

  • From Sereno: a big figure on a 2 px rule is the whole hierarchy. No tiles, no dot strings.
  • The good window is promoted to the line under the rule, because "when" is the next question after "how good".
  • The rest is label over value, which clears the ` · ` item in #644.
  • Not taken from Sereno: its sentence under the rule. A reading is a label and a value, never a sentence (readings.md, 2026-09-21). Also kept out: the painted, grainy sun, because it is decoration and colour here means a score.

Cost: small. Restyles the hero; no data change.

3 · Hour tape

  • From Weather: the day's hours slide under a fixed needle, and the reading above follows the hour you land on. Try it: drag the bars, or focus them and use the arrow keys.
  • Leads with when, which is the question a climber asks after "is it good". The good window is the bright bars, so "good 4–11 PM" is shown, not written.
  • Each bar is that hour's score in its score colour, so the tape doubles as a chart. It overlaps the Hourly chart lower down; if this wins, Hourly could start collapsed.
  • Kept out: the photo sky, the momentum fling and the auto-run.

Cost: medium. Hourly scores are already on /hourly; the tape is a new component with scroll-snap and keyboard handling.

4 · Sun on the wall

  • The most crag-specific hero: where the sun is right now and what that does to each side. Red Wing only, since it is the one crag with wall data.
  • Two scores side by side, the best sun wall and the best shade wall, each named, so the 10/19 case (100 overall, Winter Wall unbearable) cannot hide.
  • The arc and the yellow bar draw when the sun is on the leading sun wall; sunWarmingC and the walls' sun hours are already published.
  • This reopens a decision: on 2026-11-02 you chose one tappable line under the hero (idea 4, overview-34.html). This moves sun and shade into the hero itself. Rule 1 still applies: within about 10 points, crags show variant 1 or 2 instead.
  • Loose from Grain sun and Sereno: a sun that moves across the day, drawn flat.

Cost: medium, and it only covers crags with walls. Crags without walls need one of the other heroes too.
