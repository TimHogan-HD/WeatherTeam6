Opened hour, two rows

An opened hour's figures span the whole row, edge to edge, in two rows. Pick one.

A · 4 + 4 tiles

  • Row 1: Temperature, Humidity, Dew point, Score. Row 2: Chance, Rain, Sun, Wind.
  • Gusts and direction move into the Wind tile's note ("gusts 9 · SW"), so eight tiles fill two rows of four exactly.
  • Each tile about 85 px wide on this phone; figures at 21 px.

B · 5 + 4 tiles

  • All nine figures keep their own tile; Score spans two cells so row 2 fills.
  • Tiles are about 67 px wide, so "Chance of rain" becomes "Chance" and figures drop to 19 px. Tightest of the three.

C · two bands

  • Same eight figures as A, but one raised strip per row split by hairlines instead of eight boxes.
  • Least visual noise: reads as one block under the hour rather than a grid of buttons.

Same in all three

  • The detail starts at the row's left edge (under "12 PM"), not indented under the score column.
