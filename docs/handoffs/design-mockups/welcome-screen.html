Welcome screen

Rough mockup for issue #543. Drag the slider ends, tap a day, switch units and tabs.

  • Saved to the account: units, climbing hours, temperature range, and the tab a crag opens on. All four are existing settings.
  • Demo: the crag and its sample week, labelled as an example.
  • Sliders: one bar each, with a start and an end handle. Hours keep at least 3 hours between the ends; the range keeps at least 10 °F.
  • Shown when: every account that has not finished or skipped it, on its next open. Skip counts as done; Profile can run it again.
  • Install section hides when the app is already opened from the home screen.
  • Scores here follow the app's day rule (the best 3 hours in a row inside your hours) with a simplified temperature and drying model. The built version scores the sample week with the real scorer.
  • Sample week: Thu cold, Fri in range, Sat warm (low on the defaults, high once you raise the top of the range), Sun hot (helped by an earlier start), Mon rain into the afternoon (helped by a later finish).
