Palette roles, and the fold stack

The fold stack from the Construct Design library, rebuilt in plain HTML as the Overview's Right now, in three colour treatments next to today's rows. The treatments differ on one thing: how much colour a reading owns. The notes hold a palette proposal that any of them would sit on. Taylors Falls figures are the 2026-11-23 screenshot; humidity and the 24-hour chance of rain are examples.

A · Folds, library colours

  • As the library draws it: each reading owns a deep, saturated card, white type, lime dots.
  • It breaks the score ladder. The lime meter is good's hue, the brick Ground card reads as poor, the orange Air card as fair. On this screen a colour would no longer mean a score. There are only so many hues left once green, amber and red are taken.
  • It also puts four saturated slabs under a lime 95: the loudest thing on the screen stops being the answer.

Shown so the choice is concrete. Not recommended.

B · Folds, tinted

  • Each reading's own hue, the one its charts already use: rain periwinkle, wind teal, ground tan, air pale grey. The open fold takes a 16% wash of it; the closed ones stay neutral.
  • The name is set in the hue too, so Rain is the same blue here as on the Precip tab.
  • Colour stays away from the score ladder; lime is still only a score.

Cost: a new component with tab keyboard handling; no data change. Humidity and the 24-hour chance would need fields the Overview does not read today.

C · Folds, tonal

  • The folds are surface steps, not colours: card closed, raised open. The reading's hue is only its dot and its meter, small and at full strength (the library's own rule: 10% colour, in small objects).
  • The quietest of the three, and the closest to #644's direction.

Cost: as B.

What changes in all three folds
  • Every closed fold still shows its reading, so the glance is kept; only the detail waits for a tap.
  • A meter appears only where the big value is itself a fraction: here, only Rain's chance. Wind, dryness and temperature have no honest 0 to 1, so they get none; a meter on wind would be a plausible number with nothing behind it (defect class 1). Humidity is a fraction, but a meter beside 29°F would read as the temperature's.
  • Opening a tab moves to a link under the stack (Open Rain), because a link inside a fold button is a nested control (defect class 6).
  • Taken out from the library: autoplay, sound, the walking choice.

Palette roles

A proposal for colorsV2: every colour belongs to one role, and a reading's hue is the same on every screen. Today there are 43 colour tokens, 24 of them saturated, and one hue does three jobs: #7fd1c4 is wind, tempBarCool and dewMargin.

  • 20 base colours. Everything else becomes a step of one of them: rainLine, the capsule ends and the precip steps are steps of rain; gust a step of wind; rangeFrost a step of the air line.
  • One hue, one job: the Daily bar's cool end and the dew-margin line get their own steps rather than wind's teal.
  • Kept on purpose: the score ladder, per-series chart hues, the model-agreement dots, the rock swatches (decoration, outside the palette).

Cost: a token PR that renames and derives; every screen moves at once, so it goes after you pick, with check:ui screenshots before and after.
