Field guide direction

Three screens of one crag, redrawn against the AI-design tells in #637, in light and dark (switch above). Figures are sample data from a check:ui run. A starting point to react to, not a decision.

  • Glass only on the dock. Content sits on the page, split by space and hairlines, not a card per section (Apple HIG: no glass in the content layer).
  • No template chrome. Sentence-case headings, no tracked caps, no A · B strings, no › links, no glow.
  • Colour with one meaning each: green is a score or good hours, blue is rain or dew point, ochre is minutes of sun, ink is everything else.
  • One face, Public Sans, for its kinship with US government map and NWS lettering.

Daily

  • One row per day, no card. Score, low-to-high bar and chance of rain on the first line; the rest as label and value pairs under it, in place of a · string.
  • The temperature bar is one ink colour on the week's scale, not a cool-to-warm gradient.
  • A forecast that disagrees shows as its range (Sun 5 to 8 h) rather than a coloured "forecasts differ" tag.

Hourly

  • Day picker as an underlined row, not seven boxed chips.
  • The readout is a line of label and value above each chart, not a pill over it.
  • Humidity and cloud move to a printed row every three hours under their charts. Humidity is a percentage and does not share the °F axis.
  • Good hours are a green rule under the temperature line, not a tinted block over it.
  • Rain keeps its block columns and inch scale; a dry day shows its zeros.
