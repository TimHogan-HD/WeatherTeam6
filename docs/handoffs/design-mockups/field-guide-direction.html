Field guide direction

One screen, the crag Overview, redrawn against the AI-design tells in #637. Figures are sample data from a check:ui run. A starting point to react to, not a decision.

  • Glass only on the dock. Content sits on the page, split by space and hairlines, not a card per section (Apple HIG: no glass in the content layer).
  • No template chrome. Sentence-case headings, no tracked caps, no A · B strings, no › links, no glow.
  • Readings as a label and value table, not a stat grid beside a giant number.
  • The score beside its hour, then the Today chart with scores on its time axis.
  • Colour with one meaning each: green ink is a score, blue is rain or dew point, ink is everything else, buttons included.
  • One face, Public Sans, for its kinship with US government map and NWS lettering.
  • Light topo-map ground with faint contour lines. The app has no light theme yet; the same rules carry to a dark one.
