Hybrid, clean glass

Dark liquid glass on a still background. Nothing moves behind the cards; things in front move only when a reading changes.

This round. The live app’s features are back. Rock is the app’s field guide again: the stone texture, drying after rain and strength when wet, how it formed and where the holds come from, rain and sun, rock care, and this crag’s facts with Wrong rock type?. The drying clock and the 48-hour condensation chart sit above the guide. Crag is drawn: the OpenBeta area, grades, walls, a wall’s week and routes, and a route’s logbook (to-do, tick, your ticks). Trips is drawn: tap Trips in the app bar. Also back: last and next rain, how the score works, sources, Feedback, rain amount and peak wind on Daily, hours with rain and the running total on Precip, and Agreement (the app does have it; I was wrong to drop it). Air and dew now sit on one line.

  • Locked 2026-10-19: Overview uses the score bars, and the chart glow is Soft.
  • Scores are plain numbers. The big score counts to its new value when you switch crags.
  • The hero shrinks into the header. Leave Overview and the big score flies up into the slim header; come back and it grows out again.
  • The header follows the tab: the best day on Daily, the hour you are reading on Hourly, chance of rain today on Precip, the margin above dew point on Rock.
  • Tabs float at the bottom in a glass dock within thumb reach.
  • A crag is a screen you drill into. Tap a crag and the app bar slides away while the crag's dock rises in its place. The back arrow, or a swipe in from the left edge, brings the app bar back. Only Conditions is drawn; the other app tabs do nothing here.
  • A wavy drying clock on Overview, Precip and Rock fills to the hours since real rain against the rock's drying window, with a tick where the window starts.
  • Daily: seven days with the best three-hour window and temperature bars. Hourly: humidity on the temperature chart against its own right-hand scale; chance and amount of rain on one chart. Precip: last rain, today, this week, dry again, every hour of the past week as a grid, the next 48 hours, and the wettest hour each day.
  • Trips: the list soonest first with when the forecast opens; a several-crag trip as a crag-by-day grid; one crag's trip as day tiles with Agreement, the rain over the trip with its range, and how the forecast has moved (by day or whole trip); an ended trip's look back; new and edit trip; delete with a second tap.
  • Not drawn: Map, Profile, Add a crag and Feedback keep their current screens; their buttons say so.

Same rules as the app: chance of rain is the share of wet ensemble members; percentiles shade one hour and are never summed; without a score the air line is neutral ink; an alert withholds the score and keeps every reading; no friction word. Air and wind are ICON, rain the four-model median. Figures, trips and guidebook routes are sample data; crag names are real.
