What this is

A first sketch of what you described, to react to. It is not a decision and nothing in the app changes. The look borrows the field-guide direction from #644 so the old styling doesn't distract. Judge what is on the screen, not the colours.

Your points, and where they went

  • "Where can I climb today?" The home screen is your crags sorted best first.
  • Trust needs a reason, not just a number. Every crag shows its two or three deciding facts in words you can check against your own sense of the weather. The score stays, but small and beside its hour.
  • Temperature and last rain, always. They are on every row and at the top of every crag.
  • Cold days: temperature, wet rock and snow. Humidity, dew point and wind are left off, and a line says so, so you know they weren't forgotten.
  • Hot days: dew point, humidity, sun and shade appear because the weather makes them matter. This triggers on the actual temperature, not the month, so a muggy November day or a cold June morning still gets the right facts.
  • Wind only when it matters: at Red Rock because the gusts are strong, at Indian Creek because it is helping with the humidity.
  • All your data stays. The five current tabs sit under "All the data", unchanged.

Where this runs into the current rules

  • No climbing opinions (no go / don't go). Kept. No row says "good" or "go". The sorting and the reasons do that work.
  • A reading is a label and a value, never a sentence. Mostly kept: "Dew point: 58°F, muggy". The small second lines are close to sentences. Is that the tone you want, or do you want plainer?
  • New rule needed: hide a factor when it isn't in play, and say what was hidden. The cutoffs would come from the score's own humidity and wind factors.
  • Red Rock with wet sandstone shows its low score, as the app scores it today. Open question for you: should a crag whose rock reads wet hide its score and show only "Wet sandstone"? That would be a new rule, not an existing one.

What to look for

  • Could a partner who has never seen the app pick a crag from the home screen in a few seconds?
  • Would you trust it enough to skip checking Precip first? If not, what would you still go and look for?
  • Is anything missing that you always check? Anything here you'd never look at?
